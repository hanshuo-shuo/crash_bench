标签 · 做不成怎么证明LABELS · PROVING INFEASIBILITY

怎么证明“根本没有路”How to prove there is no path at all

Sihui Li, Neil T. Dantam. Towards General Infeasibility Proofs in Motion Planning. IROS 2020, doi, PDF; Learning Proofs of Motion Planning Infeasibility. RSS 2021, doi, PDF. 下文“2020”“2021”分别指这两篇。Below, “2020” and “2021” refer to these two papers.

想证明机器人从起点到不了终点，就在“会撞”的区域里找一道完整的墙，把起点和终点隔在两边。墙找到了，就是证明。To prove a robot cannot get from start to goal, find a complete wall inside the collision region that puts start and goal on opposite sides. Finding the wall is the proof.

打个比方An analogy

要证明一只蚂蚁走不出院子，看它爬一整天没出去不算数，也许它只是没找对方向。能算数的是：找到一圈连续的、蚂蚁翻不过去的围墙，并且确认蚂蚁在墙里，出口在墙外。这两篇论文做的，就是在机器人的“姿态空间”里找这样一圈墙。

To prove an ant cannot leave a yard, watching it crawl all day without getting out proves nothing; maybe it never tried the right way. What counts is finding a continuous fence the ant cannot climb, with the ant inside and the exit outside. These two papers look for such a fence in the robot's space of poses.

先认三个词Three terms first

C 空间C-space
位形空间。机器人每个关节的角度合起来是一个点，n 个关节就是一个 n 维空间。机器人的一段动作，就是这个空间里的一条连续曲线。Configuration space. The robot's joint angles together form one point; n joints give an n-dimensional space. A motion of the robot is a continuous curve in this space.
C_obs / C_free
C 空间里会碰撞的点叫 C_obs，不碰撞的叫 C_free。可行的运动必须整条落在 C_free 里。关节极限这类边界，2021 当成一层很薄的障碍来处理（第 3 页）。Points in C-space that collide form C_obs; the rest form C_free. A feasible motion must lie entirely in C_free. 2021 treats boundaries such as joint limits as a thin layer of obstacle (p. 3).
穿透深度Penetration depth
一个已经撞上的姿态，最少要改多少关节角才能不撞。它决定了“这一点周围多大范围肯定也撞”，是两篇论文里最常算、也最花时间的量。For a pose that already collides, the smallest change of joint angles that makes it collision-free. It tells you how large a neighborhood is guaranteed to collide too. Both papers compute it more than anything else, and it is the most expensive step.

它要解决什么：“没找到路”不算证明The problem: “no path found” is not a proof

RRT、PRM 这类采样规划器是概率完备的：只要存在一条留有余量的路，采样越多，找到它的概率越接近 1。反过来不成立。没有路的时候，它只会一直采样到超时。连论文自己的实验，判定“真的没路”用的也是“RRT-Connect 连续跑 20 分钟以上都没找到”，作者称之为高置信，不是证明（2021 第 7 页）。

Sampling planners such as RRT and PRM are probabilistically complete: if a path with some clearance exists, the probability of finding it approaches 1 as sampling continues. The converse fails. When no path exists, the planner just keeps sampling until it times out. Even the papers' own experiments define “truly no path” as “RRT-Connect found nothing in more than 20 minutes”, which the authors call high confidence, not proof (2021, p. 7).

“证明”的精确条件The exact condition for a proof

  • 2020（第 III-B 节）：一个封闭的多面体，每一块面片都完全在 C_obs 里；起点和终点一个在多面体里、一个在外面。判断“一里一外”用射线法：数起点到终点那条线段穿过了几块面片，奇数次就是一里一外。2020 (Sec. III-B): a closed polytope whose facets all lie entirely in C_obs, with one of start and goal inside and the other outside. “One in, one out” is checked by ray casting: count how many facets the start–goal segment crosses; an odd count means one is inside and one outside.
  • 2021（定义 1、命题 1）：找一个连续函数 f，让 f = 0 的那一圈封闭曲面整个落在 C_obs 里，并且 f(起点) 和 f(终点) 一正一负。命题 1：问题无解，当且仅当存在这样一个曲面。2021 (Definition 1, Proposition 1): find a continuous function f whose zero set f = 0 is a closed surface lying entirely in C_obs, with f(start) and f(goal) of opposite sign. Proposition 1: the problem is infeasible if and only if such a surface exists.

为什么这就够了：任何一条从起点到终点的连续路线，f 的值都要从负变到正，中间一定经过 0（介值定理）。f = 0 的点都在 C_obs 里，所以这条路线一定会撞。在 n 维 C 空间里，这道墙是 n−1 维的：2 维时是一条闭曲线，3 维时是一张闭曲面。

Why this suffices: along any continuous route from start to goal, f must go from negative to positive, so it passes through 0 somewhere (intermediate value theorem). Every point with f = 0 is in C_obs, so the route collides. In an n-dimensional C-space the wall is (n−1)-dimensional: a closed curve in 2-D, a closed surface in 3-D.

2020 的做法：在会撞的区域里“长球”，再拼成多面体2020: grow balls in the collision region, then assemble a polytope

  1. 改造 RRT-Connect：规划器每采到一个落在 C_obs 里的点，就交给证明这一侧。两件事交替进行。Modify RRT-Connect: whenever the planner samples a point in C_obs, hand it to the proof side. The two run interleaved.
  2. 从这个点附近长一个碰撞球：用优化找球心和半径，要求半径小于球心的穿透深度。这样整个球都在 C_obs 里。Grow a collision ball near that point: optimize its center and radius, requiring the radius to be smaller than the penetration depth at the center. The whole ball is then inside C_obs.
  3. 在球里放 n 个点，确定一块 n−1 维的面片（2 维是线段，3 维是三角形）。相邻的球共享顶点，面片就能接起来。Place n points in the ball to define one (n−1)-dimensional facet (a segment in 2-D, a triangle in 3-D). Neighboring balls share vertices, so their facets connect.
  4. 把“选哪些面片”写成一个 0/1 约束问题交给 SMT 求解器 Z3。两条约束：选中的面片要封闭，即每条边都恰好接上另一块（定理 1）；起点到终点的线段要穿过奇数块。Write “which facets to pick” as a 0/1 constraint problem for the SMT solver Z3, with two constraints: the chosen facets are closed, meaning each edge meets exactly one other facet (Theorem 1); and the start–goal segment crosses an odd number of them.
  5. Z3 找到解，就输出这个多面体当证明；找不到，就继续采样、继续长球。规划器那边如果先连通了起点和终点，就输出路径。If Z3 finds a solution, output the polytope as the proof; if not, keep sampling and growing balls. If the planner connects start and goal first, output the path.

2021 的做法：先用学习猜出整道墙，再逐块验证2021: guess the whole wall by learning, then verify it piece by piece

2020 的毛病是先堆零件再拼：机械臂实验平均要长 174 个球，每加一个球都要把约束问题从头重解。2021 换了方向。双向规划器本来就有两棵树，一棵从起点长，一棵从终点长。没有路的时候，两棵树永远连不上，它们中间一定隔着障碍。那就训练一个分类器把两棵树分开，分界面就是墙的候选。

2020's weakness is that it piles up parts and then assembles them: the arm experiments needed 174 balls on average, and each new ball meant re-solving the constraint problem from scratch. 2021 reverses the direction. A bidirectional planner already grows two trees, one from the start and one from the goal. When no path exists, the trees never connect, and some obstacle must lie between them. So train a classifier to separate the two trees; its decision boundary is the candidate wall.

  1. 学曲面：起点树的节点算一类，终点树的节点算另一类，训练 RBF 核 SVM。γ 从 1.0 开始，每次加 0.1，直到训练集一个都不分错。分界面 f(q) = 0 就是候选的墙。规划器采到的碰撞点不参与训练。作者说故意过拟合是想要的，但 γ 太大，曲面会断成几块（附录 A，图 8）。Learn the surface: start-tree nodes are one class, goal-tree nodes the other; train an RBF-kernel SVM. γ starts at 1.0 and grows by 0.1 until no training point is misclassified. The boundary f(q) = 0 is the candidate wall. Collision samples are not used for training. The authors want deliberate overfitting, but too large a γ breaks the surface into pieces (Appendix A, Fig. 8).
  2. 在曲面上取点：把规划器保存的每个碰撞点投影到 f = 0 上（约束优化，SLSQP）。只要有一个投影点不撞，就说明墙有漏洞：把这个点接到最近的树上（只接到给证明用的副本），回到第 1 步重学。Sample the surface: project each saved collision sample onto f = 0 (constrained optimization, SLSQP). If any projected point is collision-free, the wall has a hole: connect that point to the nearest tree (only in the copy used for the proof) and go back to step 1.
  3. 三角化：把曲面上的点抽稀，点间距不小于 d_min，再用切向 Delaunay 复形（GUDHI 库）连成封闭的多面体。三角化失败，也回到第 1 步。Triangulate: thin the surface points so that no two are closer than d_min, then connect them into a closed polytope with a tangential Delaunay complex (the GUDHI library). If triangulation fails, also go back to step 1.
  4. 逐块检查面片：给每块面片套一个最小的方块。方块中心的穿透深度如果大于中心到方块角的距离，整个方块都在 C_obs 里，面片也就在。不够大，就把方块切成 2n−1 个小块，只查和面片相交的那些。Check each facet: enclose it in the smallest box. If the penetration depth at the box center exceeds the distance from center to corner, the whole box is in C_obs, and so is the facet. Otherwise split the box into 2n−1 sub-boxes and check only those that intersect the facet.
  5. 补点或输出：某块面片里查出不撞的点，就把它投影回曲面，补进点集，重新三角化、再查。所有面片都通过，输出多面体当证明。Add points or output: if a free point turns up inside some facet, project it back onto the surface, add it to the point set, re-triangulate and check again. When every facet passes, output the polytope as the proof.

整个过程和规划器并行：规划器先找到路，输出路径；证明先验证完，输出证明。作者观察到，第一次学出来的曲面通常已经整个在 C_obs 里，大量时间花在验证这件事上（第 9 页）。

The whole process runs in parallel with the planner: if the planner finds a path first, output the path; if the proof is verified first, output the proof. The authors note that the first learned surface usually already lies entirely in C_obs, and most of the time goes into verifying that fact (p. 9).

  • 原说法：“2020 用 3 自由度 Jaco”Earlier: “2020 uses a 3-DOF Jaco”“3 自由度”出自 2021 原文。2020 只说“低维的 Jaco”，配图是三维 C 空间。两篇都没说用的是哪 3 个关节。“3-DOF” comes from the 2021 paper. 2020 only says “a low-dimensional Jaco” and shows a 3-D C-space. Neither paper says which three joints are used.

The old method's numbers in Table I match 2020 Fig. 3 exactly, so they appear to be reused rather than rerun.
  • 原说法：“没通过就重新学”Earlier: “if it fails, relearn”面片没通过，先在那里补点、重新三角化。只有投影点落在不撞的地方，或三角化失败，才重学 SVM。When a facet fails, points are first added there and the polytope re-triangulated. The SVM is relearned only when a projected point is free or triangulation fails.
  • 原说法：“维数再高，计算量指数增长”Earlier: “cost grows exponentially with dimension”这是作者对三角化这一步的预计：切向复形对曲面维数是指数的，对 C 空间维数是线性的，对点数是平方的。另外两步（取点、算穿透深度）作者预计能扩展。高维没有实测。面片检查每层切 2n−1 块，也随维数指数增长，这一点作者没讨论，是我们读出来的。This is the authors' expectation for the triangulation step: the tangential complex is exponential in the surface's dimension, linear in the C-space dimension and quadratic in the number of points. They expect the other two steps (sampling, penetration depth) to scale. Higher dimensions were not measured. Facet checking splits into 2n−1 pieces per level, which also grows exponentially; the authors do not discuss this, it is our reading.
  • 原说法：“找到了，就证明了无解”Earlier: “once found, infeasibility is proven”要加一个前提：穿透深度算对了。它是用局部优化（SLSQP 或局部搜索）算的，作者自己说这些“不是理论证明”（2021 第 3 页）。Add a premise: the penetration depth is computed correctly. It comes from local optimization (SLSQP or local search), which the authors say are “not theoretical proofs” (2021, p. 3).
  • 原说法：“首次做到 4 维”Earlier: “first to reach 4 dimensions”原话带两个限定：据作者所知；针对串联机械臂、三维以上的 C 空间（第 2 页）。The claim has two qualifiers: to the authors' knowledge, and for serial manipulators with C-spaces above three dimensions (p. 2).
  • 原说法：“需要完整的几何模型”Earlier: “needs full geometric models”作者没把这条列为局限，它是方法隐含的要求：要用 FCL 对每个连杆和每个障碍物算穿透深度。The authors do not list this as a limitation; it is an implicit requirement, since FCL must compute penetration depth for every link against every obstacle.

局限Limitations

  • 作者明说的：三角化是瓶颈，维数升高后占比会更大；算法能不能停下来，取决于 γ、Δγ、d_min 三个超参数，d_min 选不好三角化会失败，自动调参是未来工作（2021 第 6、8 页）。Stated by the authors: triangulation is the bottleneck and will take a larger share in higher dimensions; whether the algorithm terminates depends on three hyperparameters, γ, Δγ and d_min, and a poor d_min makes triangulation fail; automatic tuning is future work (2021, pp. 6, 8).
  • 只考虑运动学，不考虑动力学和其他约束；只处理起点、终点两个连通区域；只能配双向规划器。2020 的方法也能配单向规划器，但慢。Kinematics only, no dynamics or other constraints; only two connected regions (start and goal); works only with a bidirectional planner. The 2020 method also works with a unidirectional planner, but slowly.
  • 实验最高 4 维。真实机械臂通常有 6–7 个关节。Experiments go up to 4 dimensions. Real arms usually have 6–7 joints.

The differences matter too. They prove that the robot's body cannot get through in joint space, and the wall has to be found by an algorithm. 让网络只看初始场景的一张深度图，一步步猜“接下来做哪个动作才能成”；猜出来的方案再交给真正的轨迹优化去算，算得通才算数。Let a network look only at a depth image of the initial scene and guess, step by step, which action to take next so the task can succeed; then hand the guessed plan to real trajectory optimization, and count it only if the optimization works out.

打个比方An analogy

下棋高手看一眼棋盘，就知道哪几步值得细算，不会把所有走法都算一遍。网络是“棋感”，轨迹优化是“细算”。棋感错了，只是多算几步；最后落下的那一步一定是细算过的。

A strong chess player glances at the board and knows which moves are worth calculating, instead of calculating every move. The network is the intuition; trajectory optimization is the calculation. Wrong intuition only costs extra calculation; the move finally played has always been calculated.

它要解决什么The problem

两台 Franka Panda 机械臂，桌上几个盒子，要把指定的盒子放到红色目标点上。难在三处：目标点可能被别的盒子占着，要先挪开；盒子可能在一只手够不着的地方，要两只手交接；盒子的大小、位置、朝向都会变。

Two Franka Panda arms, a few boxes on a table, and the job of placing a given box on a red goal spot. Three things make it hard: the goal spot may be occupied by another box, which must be moved first; the box may be out of reach for one arm, so the arms must hand it over; and box sizes, positions and orientations vary.

动作只有两种。抓（grasp）：哪只手、哪个物体、4 种抓法里的哪一种。放（place）：哪只手、把物体放到桌上或目标点上。交接就是另一只手再抓一次。论文里没有“推”这个动作。

There are only two actions. Grasp: which hand, which object, and which of 4 grasp types. Place: which hand, and put the object on the table or on the goal spot. A handover is just the other hand grasping again. There is no “push” action in the paper.

方法框架叫逻辑-几何规划（LGP）：先在符号层面定一串动作，再对整串动作一起做轨迹优化（一个非线性规划，下面叫 NLP），看几何上能不能做到，比如会不会撞、够不够得着。必须整串一起优化，因为后面的动作会影响前面的轨迹：要交接，前一只手就得把盒子举到另一只手够得着的地方。

The framework is Logic-Geometric Programming (LGP): fix a sequence of actions at the symbolic level, then run trajectory optimization over the whole sequence (one nonlinear program, the NLP below) to see whether it can be done geometrically, e.g. without collision and within reach. The whole sequence must be optimized jointly because later actions shape earlier motion: for a handover, the first hand has to lift the box to where the second hand can reach it.

麻烦在数量。只按符号规则数，能到达目标的动作序列多得吓人；每一条都要解一次 NLP，几何上大多数又做不成。

The trouble is the count. By symbolic rules alone, the number of action sequences that reach the goal is huge; each needs its own NLP, and most are geometrically impossible.

网络怎么看图How the network reads the image

  • 每一步的输入有三样。动作符号用 one-hot 编码。动作-物体图有 3 个通道：初始场景的深度图、这一步涉及的物体 1 的掩码、物体 2 的掩码（抓只涉及一个物体，第二个通道全 0；放的第二个物体是桌子或目标点）。目标-物体图标出要放的盒子和目标点，每一步都输入。Three inputs per step. The action symbol is one-hot encoded. The action-object image has 3 channels: the depth image of the initial scene, a mask of object 1 in this action, and a mask of object 2 (a grasp involves one object, so the second channel is all zeros; for a place, the second object is the table or the goal spot). The goal-object image marks the box to place and the goal spot, and is fed at every step.
  • 图像始终是初始场景。网络看不到“蓝盒被挪走以后”的样子，挪没挪只能记在 GRU 的隐状态里，相当于让它自己想象动作的后果。The image is always the initial scene. The network never sees the scene after the blue box has been moved; whether it has moved can only be kept in the GRU's hidden state, so the network has to imagine the effects of its actions.
  • 输出问的是“还能不能成”：在已经选定前面几步的条件下，还存在一种接法，能凑成一条到达目标、轨迹优化也可行的完整序列的概率（式 4）。它问的不是“这一步本身能不能做”。The output asks “can this still succeed?”: given the steps chosen so far, the probability that some continuation completes a sequence reaching the goal with a feasible trajectory (Eq. 4). It does not ask whether this one step can be executed.

标签怎么来，用一个小例子走一遍Where the labels come from, with a small example

  • 生成 30,000 个两物体场景，其中一半故意把另一个盒子放在目标点上。每个场景做广度优先搜索、逐条解 NLP，找到 4 条可行序列或查完 1,000 个叶子就停（第 V-B 节）。Generate 30,000 two-object scenes; in half of them another box is deliberately placed on the goal spot.   • 至少找到一条可行序列的场景有 25,736 个，只用这些训练。里面有 102,566 条可行序列、2,741,573 条不可行序列，可行的只占约 3.6%。25,736 scenes have at least one feasible sequence, and only these are used for training. They contain 102,566 feasible and 2,741,573 infeasible sequences, so only about 3.6% are feasible.
  • 每条序列变成逐步标签（式 7）：整条可行，每一步都标 1；整条不可行，但前 j 步和某条已知可行序列一样，前 j 步标 1、后面标 0；其余都标 0。训练用加权的二元交叉熵，每批 48 条，至少 16 条来自可行序列。Each sequence becomes per-step labels (Eq. 7): a fully feasible sequence gets 1 at every step; an infeasible sequence whose first j steps match some known feasible sequence gets 1 for those j steps and 0 after; everything else gets 0. Training uses weighted binary cross-entropy with batches of 48, at least 16 of them from feasible sequences.

ICLR 2025; arXiv 2408.15240. 读的是 v3。早期版本摘要里的“16–64%”“16–40%”和终版不同，网上看到这类数字多半出自旧版。We read v3. 把“这个解答对不对”变成让模型接着写一个词：读完题目和解答，在“答案对吗（Yes/No）？”后面写 Yes 或 No，用写 Yes 的概率当分数。还可以先写一段逐条核对，再写 Yes/No；考试时多写几遍，把 Yes 的概率取平均。Turn “is this solution correct?” into asking the model to write one more word: after reading the problem and the solution, it writes Yes or No after “Is the answer correct (Yes/No)?”, and the probability of Yes is the score. It can also write a step-by-step check before the Yes/No; at test time it writes several checks and averages the Yes probabilities.

打个比方An analogy

传统的打分模型像一台读卡机：扫一眼，吐出一个分数，说不出理由。GenRM 像一位改卷老师：可以直接打勾打叉，也可以先在旁边一条条批注“这一步对、这一步错”，最后给结论；拿不准的时候，多批几遍取个平均。

A conventional reward model is like an answer-sheet scanner: one glance, one number out, no reasons. GenRM is like a teacher grading: it can simply tick or cross, or first annotate line by line (“this step is right, this one is wrong”) and then give a verdict; when unsure, grade it a few times and average.

它要解决什么The problem

它用在 Best-of-N 里：生成器对一道题写 N 个解答，验证器给每个解答打分，选分最高的那个。验证器越准，选中的越可能是对的。

It is used in Best-of-N: a generator writes N solutions to a problem, the verifier scores each, and the highest-scoring one is chosen. The better the verifier, the more likely the chosen solution is correct.

一道题→生成器写 N 个解答→验证器给每个打分→选分最高的→看选中的对不对
a problem→generator writes N solutions→verifier scores each→pick the top one→is it correct?

常用的验证器是判别式奖励模型（下面叫判别式 RM）：在大模型上接一个打分头，用二元交叉熵训练，只输出一个数。它用不上大模型本来就会的“边写边想”。GenRM 换了做法：验证就是生成，用的还是语言模型本来的训练方式。

The usual verifier is a discriminative reward model (discriminative RM below): a scoring head on a large model, trained with binary cross-entropy, that outputs one number. It cannot use the large model's ability to think in writing. GenRM does it differently: verification is generation, trained the way language models are already trained.

原理 · 为什么拆开就学得会PRINCIPLE · WHY SPLITTING HELPS

为什么拆成小步，学不会的题就学得会了Why breaking a task into small steps makes the unlearnable learnable

Noam Wies, Yoav Levine, Amnon Shashua. Sub-Task Decomposition Enables Learning in Sequence to Sequence Tasks. ICLR 2023 (poster); arXiv 2204.02892 (v4); 作者代码authors' code.

有一类题，只拿最终答案去训练，梯度几乎带不来信号，要指数级的步数才学得会。把中间步骤的答案也写进输出一起监督，每一步就只是“看前面两个数做一次异或”的小题，多项式步数就能学会。Some tasks give gradients almost no signal when trained on the final answer only, and need exponentially many steps to learn. Add the answers to intermediate steps to the output and supervise them too: each step becomes a small task of “XOR two earlier tokens”, and a polynomial number of steps suffices.

打个比方An analogy

开一把四位密码锁。如果只有四位全对时才“咔”一声，你只能一个个组合去试，最多一万次。如果每拨对一位就“咔”一声，最多四十次。只给最终答案，相当于只在全对时响一声；中间监督就是每一位的那一声。

Open a four-digit combination lock. If it clicks only when all four digits are right, you have to try combinations one by one, up to ten thousand. If it clicks each time one digit is right, forty tries at most. Supervising only the final answer is the click for all four; intermediate supervision is a click for each digit.

难题长什么样：奇偶题The hard task: parity

输入是 d 个随机的 0/1。事先随机选定其中 d/2 个位置，模型不知道是哪几个。标签是这 d/2 位里 1 的个数是奇数还是偶数，也就是把它们一路异或（XOR）下去。

The input is d random 0/1 bits. d/2 positions are chosen at random beforehand, and the model does not know which. The label is whether those d/2 bits contain an odd or even number of 1s, i.e. their XOR all the way down.

d = 8，选中的位置是 1、3、4、7（模型不知道）
x = 1 1 0 1 0 1 1 0
选中的四位：x1=1  x3=0  x4=1  x7=1  → 三个 1，奇数 → 答案 1

如果只看出其中三位（x1、x3、x4）：它们的异或是 0，
x7 是 0 还是 1 各占一半 → 猜对的概率仍是 50%
d = 8, chosen positions 1, 3, 4, 7 (unknown to the model)
x = 1 1 0 1 0 1 1 0
chosen bits: x1=1  x3=0  x4=1  x7=1  → three 1s, odd → answer 1

if only three of them are found (x1, x3, x4): their XOR is 0,
x7 is 0 or 1 with equal odds → still 50% correct

难就难在这里：漏看选中的任何一位，正确率都是 50%。认对了“大部分位置”和完全没认对一样，没有“越来越接近”的信号。数学上，不同子集的奇偶函数两两正交，所以梯度里几乎不含“目标是哪个子集”的信息（附录 F，引理 13、14）。论文据此引用 Shalev-Shwartz 等（2017）和 Shamir（2018）的结论：梯度只能算到近似精度时（误差约 e−d/3），任何网络在多项式步数内都停在瞎猜附近（推论 1）。

That is the difficulty: miss any one chosen bit and accuracy is 50%. Getting “most of the positions” right is no better than getting none, so there is no signal of getting closer. Mathematically, parities of different subsets are pairwise orthogonal, so the gradient carries almost no information about which subset is the target (Appendix F, Lemmas 13, 14). On this basis the paper cites Shalev-Shwartz et al. (2017) and Shamir (2018): when gradients can only be computed approximately (error about e−d/3), any network stays near chance within a polynomial number of steps (Corollary 1).

中间监督怎么给How intermediate supervision is given

定理说了什么What the theorem says

  • 条件：每个要预测的位置，都只依赖前面常数个位置，并且能写成一个次数低、系数有界的多项式（再取符号）。奇偶题里每一步是两个位置的异或，满足条件（引理 2）。Condition: each position to be predicted depends only on a constant number of earlier positions, and can be written as a low-degree polynomial with bounded coefficients (followed by a sign). In parity, each step is the XOR of two positions, which qualifies (Lemma 2).
  • 模型和训练：一个很宽的 ReLU 循环网络（Elman RNN），只训练循环权重，用 SGD；附录 D 证明有限精度的全梯度下降也行，这和负面结果是同一个设定。Model and training: a very wide ReLU recurrent network (Elman RNN), training only the recurrent weights, with SGD; Appendix D shows finite-precision full gradient descent also works, the same setting as the negative result.
  • 结论（定理 2 → 推论 1）：训练步数对 d 和 1/ε 是多项式，所有迭代平均下来，测试错误率低于 ε。没有中间监督时，同一类方法要指数步。Conclusion (Theorem 2 → Corollary 1): the number of training steps is polynomial in d and 1/ε, and the test error averaged over iterations is below ε. Without intermediate supervision the same class of methods needs exponentially many steps.
  • 推广（第 6 节）：任何多项式时间能算的函数，都能写成每个门只有常数个输入的电路；把每个门的值当中间答案，就能这样学会。在密码学假设下，存在这样的题：有中间监督能学会，没有时任何多项式时间算法都学不会。Generalization (Sec. 6): any function computable in polynomial time can be written as a circuit whose gates each have a constant number of inputs; using every gate's value as an intermediate answer makes it learnable in the same way. Under cryptographic assumptions, some tasks are learnable with intermediate supervision and not learnable without it by any polynomial-time algorithm.

  • 原说法：“没有人证明过有了中间监督就学得会”Earlier: “no one had proven that intermediate supervision makes it learnable”这是作者自己的说法（摘要、第 1–2 节），写的时候注明“作者称”。That is the authors' own claim (abstract, Secs. 1–2); attribute it to them.
  • 补充Addition理论是循环网络，实验是 Transformer；Transformer 只有实验，没有定理。奇偶题本身用高斯消元就能解，难只针对梯度方法，这也是为什么推广到一般任务要借助密码学假设（这一条是我们的补充）。The theory covers recurrent networks and the experiments use a Transformer; there is no theorem for Transformers. Parity itself is solvable by Gaussian elimination, so it is hard only for gradient methods, which is why the general result needs cryptographic assumptions (our addition).

局限Limitations

  • 作者明说的：要“足够多”的中间监督。奇偶题用了约 d 个中间答案，只用常数个是否也有指数级的好处，留作开放问题；哪些任务端到端就能学、哪些必须拆，还不清楚（第 7 节）。Stated by the authors: intermediate supervision must be “sufficient”. Parity uses about d intermediate answers; whether a constant number still gives an exponential advantage is left open, and which tasks are learnable end to end versus needing decomposition is unclear (Sec. 7).
  • 正面定理只覆盖一种很特殊的设定：极宽的循环网络、只训练一组权重、固定学习率。The positive theorem covers a very particular setting: an extremely wide recurrent network, training one set of weights, with a fixed learning rate.
  • 必须事先知道怎么拆，并且每一步都给监督。You must know in advance how to decompose the task, and supervise every step.
对我们的启发What it means for us

我们的判定正好是一个异或。s 是“图里把目标和终点隔开的是哪个颜色的盒子”，只能从图里看；t 是“规则保护哪个颜色”，只能从文字里读。判定 = s XOR t，在 512 道训练题上全部成立。四道题一组的平衡设计，让单看 s 或单看 t 都只能猜对一半，和奇偶题“漏看一位就是 50%”是同一个结构，只是这里只有两位。

Our verdict is exactly an XOR. s is “which color of box separates the target from the goal in the image”, readable only from the image; t is “which color the rule protects”, readable only from the text. Verdict = s XOR t, which holds on all 512 training items. The balanced four-item unit makes s alone or t alone worth only 50%, the same structure as parity's “miss one bit and you are at 50%”, except with only two bits.

  • 对得上的地方。单通路对照里，只看文字的标签 t、只看图的标签 s，16 道题都学得会，二者的异或就学不会。B 把中间答案写出来，512 道题上学得会（验证集 84.5%）。第 2 步里，在判定前加 4 个固定 token（AF）或复述颜色（AP），512 道题上都还是 50%：起作用的是中间“答案”，不是多出来的输出位置。Wies 的设定也是这样，监督的是每个子任务的值。Where it matches. In the single-pathway controls, a text-only label t and an image-only label s are each learned on 16 items, but their XOR is not. B, writing intermediate answers, is learnable on 512 items (84.5% on validation). In step 2, adding 4 fixed tokens before the verdict (AF) or restating the color (AP) still leaves 512-item training at 50%: what helps is intermediate answers, not extra output positions. Wies's setting is the same: it supervises the value of each sub-task.
  • 对不上的地方。两位的异或在 Wies 的框架里本身是容易的一步（只依赖 2 个位置）。我们的理解是，难在一步之内既要从图像里读出关键的那一位，又要和文字组合。我们用的是预训练的 Qwen2.5-VL 加 LoRA，输入是图像，和它“从头训练的宽 RNN、输入是比特”差得很远。所以这个定理只能当先例，不能当解释；我们也没有测梯度。Where it does not. A two-bit XOR is itself an easy step in Wies's framework (it depends on only 2 positions). Our reading is that the difficulty lies in reading the key bit out of an image and combining it with the text within a single step. We use a pretrained Qwen2.5-VL with LoRA on image inputs, far from a wide RNN trained from scratch on bits. So the theorem is a precedent, not an explanation, and we have not measured gradients.

同一个动作，换一个对象就可能从安全变成危险。HazardArena 给每个危险任务配一个动作完全一样的安全“孪生”，用来分清机器人是懂得“不该做”，还是只是“做不到”。The same motion can turn dangerous when one object changes. 局限Limitations

  • 标签是人定的语义类别，危险不危险靠常识，没有证明。这对它的问题是合理的，但和我们“两类标签都有证明”不是一回事。Labels are human-defined semantic categories (whether something is dangerous is common sense), with no proof. That suits its question, but it is not the same as our proofs for both labels.
  • 主实验只有 10 对孪生，每类样本不多，所有比例都没有置信区间。The main experiments use only 10 twin pairs; each category has few samples, and no proportion comes with a confidence interval.
