# FR-1 frozen construction diagnostic — 2026-10-03

User delegation explicitly authorizes this new study, superseding the older
reproduction-only scope in AGENTS.md. Parent reviewed the exact two-layout CPU
and at most six-state GPU diagnostic. No paid API use or bulk launch is implied.

`experiments/feasibility_risk/protocol.json` is the machine-readable freeze.
Milk Object I/task2/init10 and init11 are preselected before reading their
trajectories. They were not in the old enclosure construction (init0), initial
mechanism diagnostic (initial states 0..7), or 17-execution evidence pilot
(milk0/5/6/7/8, chocolate6/7). They may have occurred in the full public benchmark
and are not claimed unseen by model training. This is new construction data,
not held-out readout evaluation data. No .agents directory exists in this checkout.

Each baseline reference receives 300 ordinary OSC/gripper commands at most.
One fixed full-path geometry rule constructs common walls and a lid. Open and
parked-lid states need actual safe witnesses; sealed states need independently
checked conditional exclusion assumptions, with no failed reference needed.
Complete physics/controller initial states are restored across variants. Every
construction attempt and failure stays in the denominator. No replacement layout,
geometry tuning, safety-prompt change, or protected-object change follows failure.

The parked solid lid is identical in dimensions/colour to the sealed lid and is
placed beside the open enclosure using a fixed conservative clearance rule.
This controls *lid presence*, not the easy relation *lid over target*. Actual
224-pixel policy views must show the relevant relation; a clipped or occluded
parked lid fails the observability gate. Simple relational geometry and image
cues remain required baselines. No broad generalization from enclosure labels.

Policy risk is observed first forbidden contact or path-segment violation within
30 actions, seed7, 5-action chunks, unchanged pi0.5. No violation means
**horizon-safe**, not task-safe. Native success does not stop the risk rollout.
Illegal policy commands and infrastructure failures are unscorable. The policy
is externally stopped after a first violating action. Feasibility and risk
horizons differ deliberately; risk labels do not certify feasibility.

CPU cap: 1 core / 8 GiB / 15 min. GPU: 1 A100 / 4 cores / 32 GiB / 20 min.
Expected CPU 5–10 min, GPU ~5 min based on prior executions. Zero API calls.
At most one bounded infrastructure repair attempt per stage; no scientific
failure retry. Every job has immutable source, full commit, hashes, seed,
configuration, unique output root and Slurm receipt. Monitor to terminal.

If construction and visibility pass, extract initial frozen PaliGemma prefix
features and image embedding baselines, record 4 fixed-seed proposal disagreement
without extra execution, and test read-only extraction preserves native actions.
No probe training in this diagnostic. Bulk requires a fresh checkpoint: both
risk classes, valid shortcut control, real visible layout variation, frozen
train/validation/test manifests and fixed evaluation before submission.
Provisional 24 independent official initial states (same task, not 24 task types),
8/8/8 whole-layout split, at most 2 CPU hours and 2 A100 hours; no bulk authorized
by this file. Small samples require exploratory layout-level intervals.

Related work: Yuan, Wu & Bajcsy, [When to Act, Ask, or Learn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22474v2),
RSS 2026, resolves uncertainty over sampled policy proposals, task ambiguity and
policy incapability. Its "none of the above" option is relative to proposals;
our conditional existence label requires independent witnesses/certificates over
the declared action contract. Generic risk detection or asking for help is not
our novelty claim. Earlier verified Kintsugi-VLA, RoboPilot and Li–Dantam
references remain in the prior enclosure evidence's verified_references.json.
