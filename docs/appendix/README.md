# Appendix index

These documents support the paper but are not its narrative entrypoints.

- [GLASS_SAFETY_UTILITY.md](GLASS_SAFETY_UTILITY.md): compact interpretation of
  glass dose response, prompt behavior, exact-state Oracle counterfactuals, and
  the learned D/E/F gate failure; it also maps Broad B, strict authoring,
  restore/hash, and Slurm evidence to the frozen source records.
- [CAREFUL_PROMPT_EXPERIMENT.md](CAREFUL_PROMPT_EXPERIMENT.md): complete frozen
  E13 prompt protocol and result matrix.
- [P0_EXPERIMENT.md](P0_EXPERIMENT.md): E12 hazard-validity/generalization
  protocol and its negative/indeterminate outcome.
- [NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md](NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md): concise index of failed, blocked,
  or non-identifiable studies.

Threshold sweeps, π0 tap details, steering diagnostics, witness results, and
other result-level appendices remain under `results/ANALYSIS_*.md` so their
manifest and historical links stay stable.

Superseded plans and chronological execution records are not appendix truth
sources; they are in the [legacy archive](../archive/README.md).
